# Hide chats on this device

Open a direct or group chat and choose **Hide on this device** above the chat header. It disappears from the regular Chats list and its search results. Choose **Hidden chats** above the sidebar search to find it. Open it there and choose **Restore to chats** to bring it back.

Choices are saved in account-scoped IndexedDB and survive reopening Locat. Incoming messages, offline delivery and importing history do not automatically restore hidden chats. History is retained; message delivery and notification preferences continue normally. Another account on this browser has separate choices.

Hiding is organization, not authentication or encryption at rest. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser session can open Hidden chats. Choices apply only to this browser/device and are not included in exported chat backups or server metadata backups. Clearing site data also clears these choices and local history.

Device acceptance: hide a direct and group chat, search the regular list, reopen the app, receive another message, open the Hidden chats view and restore each chat. Confirm history remains and an unrelated account cannot inherit the choice. Repeat in mobile Firefox and Chrome.
